Fix YouTube Volume — Logarithmic Volume Scaling for YouTube

# Now open source!

---

# Overview:

YouTube's default volume control is linear, which doesn't align well with how humans perceive changes in loudness. This extension fixes that by applying a logarithmic volume scaling to YouTube's player, making volume adjustments feel more natural and smooth.

---

# Features:

- Converts YouTube's volume slider to logarithmic scale for better perceptual control.
- Works seamlessly with YouTube's single-page application (SPA) navigation, so you won't even notice it.
- Remembers your adjusted volume during your session.
- Lightweight and minimal.
- Automatically detects new videos and applies logarithmic scaling without user intervention.
- Settings GUI (added in v1.6) for adjusting the strength of logarithmic scaling (most users won't need this).

---

# Why logarithmic volume?

Human hearing perceives loudness logarithmically, meaning equal increments in volume are not perceived as equal changes in loudness. This extension attempts to bridge that gap, matching volume control to how we hear, making it easier to set comfortable listening levels.
